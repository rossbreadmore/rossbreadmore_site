1. 01 2024 — Present

    JPMorgan Chase

    Executive Director, User Experience · London

    JPMorgan hired me to lead experience and design for compliance technology — the tools risk and control teams rely on every day inside one of the world's largest banks. The starting point: legacy, fragmented tooling, and a culture where design had rarely been treated as a lever for business outcomes.

    I'm building a progressive team from the ground up and staying hands-on with the work myself, not just reviewing it from the sidelines — setting a new bar for craft in a part of the bank that had never had one, and using it to change how a global institution thinks about the tools its people use every day.

  2. 02 2021 — 2024

    4G Capital

    Chief Product Officer · London & Nairobi

    4G Capital is Africa's leading neobank and a certified B Corp, serving customers and small businesses across Kenya and Uganda that mainstream banks overlook. When I joined, there was no dedicated product function, and the technology stack was holding the business back.

    I built a product team from scratch, modernised the platform, and shipped new digital services for colleagues and customers across East Africa — hiring and mentoring a team capable of moving fast inside a mission-driven business that takes financial access seriously.

  3. 03 2016 — 2021

    Lloyds Banking Group

    Product & Service Design · London

    Lloyds hired me to build a service design capability that didn't exist yet, inside one of the UK's largest banks. Every product line — mortgages, current accounts, everything in between — needed a consistent, higher standard of design.

    I grew the team to 34 designers, then became design director for retail, shaping a wider team of 100 and setting the vision for what great looked like across every surface. I finished by product-owning a new digital service end to end, then ran a 'run-ahead' team using emerging tech to solve problems customers hadn't been able to name yet.
